## How to read this register

- **Materiality base.** The Sep-24 (period `202409`) reported provision is
  **£8,975,618.47** total ECL on £364,013,988.03 of EAD, per
  `data/expected/ecl_by_segment_202409.csv`, which is the legacy engine's captured
  production output for that period and the migration parity baseline. All percentages are
  of that group ECL figure.
- **Confidence in the figures.** The 20 macros the batch executes were independently
  reimplemented from the code as written and reproduce the baseline to £0.0024 on total ECL
  and £0.01 on total EAD, at segment and stage level across all 18 rows
  ([07](../discovery/07_evidence.md)). The measured impacts below are therefore impacts on
  the actual reported number, not estimates. Items marked *not quantified* are read from the
  code and are unexercised by the sample period.
- **Direction** is stated from the perspective of the reported provision: *understates* means
  the published figure is lower than the stated policy would produce.
- **Part A** holds findings whose root cause and correct treatment can both be established
  from the repository. **Part B** holds findings that cannot be closed by reading code: they
  need a fact from outside the repository (Collections, Economics, the Provisions Committee,
  the upstream data owner or Infra Batch Services) before the decision can be taken.
- **Restatement** column answers only: does acting on this finding call a *published* number
  into question? The materiality judgment itself is the Audit Committee's.
